Tor Browser is for the whole web. OnionzWeb is for onion sites.

The official Tor Browser is the right choice for most people. OnionzWeb is narrower: an onion-only browser with its own private search index built in.

What OnionzWeb does not do

It does not visit normal websites. It is a development build for 64-bit Android only, not an official Tor Browser release, and not independently audited. Its engine updates come only with new OnionzWeb builds.
