Privacy policy

OnionzWeb has no servers and no accounts. Your index, your searches and your browsing stay on your phone and on Tor.

In short

The developer collects nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, in the app or on this website. Tor Browser's own telemetry and update checks are switched off.

Who the app talks to

  • The Tor network, to connect. Tor relays see that you use Tor.
  • The onion sites you crawl or visit, through Tor. A site you visit can see what you do on it, as with any browser.

Normal websites are blocked. Opening a downloaded file in another app can bypass Tor and reveal your internet address; the app warns you before it does.

What stays on your phone

The index, sources, bookmarks and settings are encrypted on the phone and are not included in phone backups. Uninstalling or clearing the app's data deletes them.

Children

OnionzWeb is not for children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 21 November 2026.
